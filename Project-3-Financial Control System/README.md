# Excel-Based Cash Flow & Financial Control System

## 📌 Overview
A robust financial management tool designed for startups and small agencies to bridge the gap between "having money" and "being profitable." This system automates income/expense tracking, calculates monthly burn rates, and provides a 12-month cash flow forecast.

## 🔴 The Problem
Small business owners often struggle with:
- Fragmented data across different bank accounts.
- Not knowing their "True Profit" after hidden expenses.
- Uncertainty about future cash positions (Runway).

## 🛠️ Key Features
- **Dynamic Dashboard:** Real-time visualization of Profit vs. Loss and Expense distribution.
- **Automated Summaries:** Uses advanced `SUMIFS` and Date Logic to aggregate transactions by month and category.
- **Cash Flow Forecasting:** Predictive modeling based on recurring expenses and pending invoices.
- **Burn Rate Calculator:** Instant calculation of business longevity ($Current Cash / Monthly Expenses$).

## 🔧 Technical Skills Used
- **Advanced Formulas:** `SUMIFS`, `EOMONTH`, `IFERROR`, and Nested Logic.
- **Data Architecture:** Implemented Data Validation and Table structures to ensure data integrity.
- **Pivot Tables & Slicers:** Interactive filtering by time period, category, or payment status.
- **Financial Modeling:** Visualizing "Cash-on-Hand" vs. "Accrual-based Profit."
